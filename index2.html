Closed Businesses in Oakland Chinatown

This map is part of my reporting project examining how concerns about crime and public safety are affecting small businesses in Oakland Chinatown, including whether businesses have closed or shortened their hours.
To explore the broader pattern of business closures, I reviewed the City of Oakland’s closed business account records from 2022 to 2026, manually checked business locations and closure status, and mapped the businesses I could confirm had closed in Chinatown. Click on each dot to view details.

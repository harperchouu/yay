Campaign Finance Data: Who is funding Oakland’s mayoral candidates?

by Harper Chou

Oakland voters are going to choose their next mayor this November.

There are four candidates running for mayor in the 2026 local election, including current Mayor Barbara Lee, educator Mindy Pechenuk, business owner Brenda Grisham, and community investment executive Julius Robinson.

While Robinson has no contribution records in the direct monetary contributions dataset, the other three candidates are drawing campaign money from very different donor bases.

The reporting team analyzed campaign finance data and calculated the total direct monetary contributions received by each mayoral candidate. The earliest contribution record found dates back to March 2025, and the totals are calculated through June 30, 2026.

Barbara Lee was elected after former Mayor Sheng Thao was recalled. At an Oct. 3 candidate forum, Lee said public safety would be one of her top priorities if elected. She pledged to recruit more police officers under Measure NN and continue addressing homelessness and illegal dumping in Oakland.

Not only does Barbara Lee receive the most contributions, she also has the most diverse group of contributors, including committees and businesses.

Committee contributions to Barbara Lee include the Alameda Labor Council PAC, Amalgamated Transit Union Local, American Federation of State, County and Municipal Employees, Council 57 PAC, Bay Area Rapid Transit AFSCME Local 3993, and Engineers and Scientists of California. The “Other” category contributors include businesses such as Alameda Sushi House and Turbo Auto Body, Inc.

Pechenuk received all of her direct monetary contributions from individual donors. At the candidate forum, she identified public safety as one of her priorities and emphasized addressing drug-related crime, including drug cartels and open-air drug markets.

Half of Grisham’s contribution dollars fall under the “Other” category, which includes businesses such as Cafe Aduu LLC and First Aid Transportation LLC. Public safety is also one of her top priorities. Grisham has been a victims’ rights advocate since her son, Christopher LaVell Jones, was killed outside their East Oakland home in 2010. His killing remains unsolved.

According to a report by FOX, the fourth candidate, Julius Robinson, said his campaign team lacked infrastructure and leadership. The Oaklandside also reported that it was unable to find information about Robinson or his campaign team.

Where are the contributions from?

Among the three candidates, most of their direct monetary contributions come from California, with only a small share coming from outside the state, including Washington, D.C., Massachusetts and Maryland.

Within California, most of the contributions come from Oakland.

With the election approaching, candidates have received support from people and organizations across the United States. On September 3, Barbara Lee announced that former Vice President Kamala Harris had endorsed her reelection campaign for Oakland mayor.

Other candidates have also listed endorsements on their websites. Brenda Grisham’s endorsements include business executives and Bay Area rapper Kamaiyah Johnson.

Mindy Pechenuk’s website highlights three endorsements, including state controller candidate Herb Morgan, the GOP Union Caucus, and former State Sen. Mike Morrell.

Campaign Finance Data: Who is funding Oakland’s mayoral candidates?

by Yuan-Hsi Chou

Oakland voters are going to choose their next mayor this November.

There are four candidates running for mayor in the 2026 local election, including current Mayor Barbara Lee, educator Mindy Pechenuk, business owner Brenda Grisham, and community investment executive Julius Robinson.

While Robinson has no contribution records in the direct monetary contributions dataset, the other three candidates are drawing campaign money from very different donor bases.

The reporting team analyzed campaign finance data obtained from Oakland Open Data on Sept. 10. The earliest contribution record found dates back to March 2025, and the analysis includes direct monetary contributions reported through June 30, 2026.

Barbara Lee was elected after former Mayor Sheng Thao was recalled. Under Lee’s administration, Oakland has continued to see declines in violent crime in 2026. She has also made it clear that public safety would remain one of her campaign priorities, including recruiting more police officers under Measure NN.

Not only does Barbara Lee receive the most contributions, she also has the most diverse group of contributors, including committees and businesses.

Committee contributions to Barbara Lee include the Alameda Labor Council PAC, Amalgamated Transit Union Local, American Federation of State, County and Municipal Employees, Council 57 PAC, Bay Area Rapid Transit AFSCME Local 3993, and Engineers and Scientists of California. The “Other” category contributors include businesses such as Alameda Sushi House and Turbo Auto Body, Inc.

Individual donors are also pouring money into Oakland’s mayoral race. Pechenuk received all of her direct monetary contributions from individual donors, most of whom were retired or unemployed. Her campaign has emphasized addressing drug-related crime, and she has also voiced strong support for data center development in Oakland.

What are the top occupations among individual contributors?

Grisham received half of her contribution dollars from individuals and half from donors in the “Other” category. The “Other” category includes businesses such as Cafe Aduu LLC and First Aid Transportation LLC, while occupations listed by individual donors include CEO and program manager. Grisham has been a longtime advocate for victims’ rights since her son, Christopher LaVell Jones, was killed outside their East Oakland home in 2010. She has also made improving public safety in Oakland a key focus of her campaign.

As of Sept. 10, the reporting team was unable to find much publicly available information about Robinson, including his official campaign website. This section will be updated as more information becomes available.

Where are the contributions from?

Among the three candidates, most of their direct monetary contributions come from California, with only a small share coming from outside the state, including Washington, D.C., Massachusetts and Maryland.

Within California, most of the contributions come from Oakland.

Top cities for direct monetary contributions

Barbara Lee drew substantial contributions from a broader range of California cities. In addition to about $118,000 from Oakland, she received about $33,000 from San Francisco and $13,000 from Berkeley. For Mindy Pechenuk and Brenda Grisham, contributions from cities outside Oakland were much smaller.

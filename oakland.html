Who is directly contributing to Oakland’s mayoral candidates?

Oakland mayoral candidates are drawing campaign money from very different donor bases. While Barbara Lee receives support from both individuals and committees, many of them labor related, her opponents rely mostly on individual donors. The differences in their donor bases can also reveal differences in the candidates’ support networks.

Note: The data comes from Oakland Opendata, I reached out to the Public Ethics Commission to confrim the reliability of the dataset. In this analysis, I used schedule A to calculate monetary contributions.
